About this Database

  • Explore English word families, compound words, basewords, roots, prefixes, suffixes, and frequency measures.
  • The BAWF database is designed to support vocabulary, morphology, and word-family instruction.
  • Morphology is the study of how words are built from meaningful units, including bases, roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Use the curated tab to focus on familiar and high-utility word families for teaching.
  • Use the full database tabs to explore the broader lexicon attested in large English corpora.
  • Use sorting, filtering, AI explanation prompts, dictionary links, and etymology links to explore entries.
  • Download the raw compressed CSV files for offline use and advanced analysis.
  • Use the Word Cloud tab to create visuals from word-frequency lists.

Be inspired by joyful word learning and reading — dive in and explore!

Curated Word Family List: Frequent and Familiar Words

This curated word family list contains frequent and familiar word families that most people know and that cover much of the text children read.

The full database is for exploring all words attested in the largest current corpora. The focused database is a trimmed version that lists only word families with familiarity ratings above approximately 3.5, suggesting that these words are widely known by most speakers of English. These words are likely to be more useful for teaching than words that are not well known by English-language speakers.

This filtered spreadsheet does not contain all database information. It includes the basewords and members of familiar word-family derivations. It lists word-family size and word-family frequency differently than the main database because these measures are computed only among familiar items. This makes sense instructionally because word-family size can mean how many words in a family English speakers typically know.

Column Definitions

Core Word Information

  • Word: The surface wordform.
  • Word_frq_Zipf / Word_frq_Zipf_Recomputed: Zipf frequency score of the wordform based on BNC/COCA frequency data. Higher values mean more frequent words.
  • FLemma: Lemma or dictionary-style headword when available.
  • GPT_Coded_Word_Parts: GPT-assisted morphological breakdown of the word.

Baseword Information

  • Baseword: The central baseword of a word family.
  • Baseword_frq_Zipf: Word-family frequency computed from the summed frequencies of family members.
  • Baseword_Family_Size: Number of different known wordforms in the baseword family.

Roots, Prefixes, and Suffixes

  • GPT_Root: Main root identified by GPT-assisted morphological analysis.
  • Greek_Latin_Root_1: First identified classical root when available.
  • Prefix_1 to Prefix_3: Prefixes identified in the word.
  • Suffix_1 to Suffix_6: Suffixes identified in the word.

Educational Measures

  • N_Morphemes_from_GPT_parse_without_inflections: Morpheme count excluding inflectional morphemes.
  • N_Morphemes_from_GPT_parse_plus_inflections: Morpheme count including inflectional morphemes.
  • Reading AoA Green et al._ 2025: Estimated reading age of acquisition.
  • Vocabulary eXpected Grade Level Flor et al._ 2024: Estimated grade level at which the word is known.
  • GPT_Familiarity Brysbaert et al._ 2024: GPT-estimated familiarity rating when available.
